WHY THE LAST TEN HURTS

The build is cheap. The fiddling is where it bleeds.

A crew gets you a working site fast. Then you spend a fortune in tokens asking a model to move a button four pixels, undo it, try a warmer red. Designs takes that part off the model and puts it back in your hands, where a nudge is just a nudge.

# the old way · one nudge, one round-trip you > move the CTA up a little, warmer red, tighter gap model > regenerating section · 3.2k tokens · guesses # designs · you just move it canvas > drag · recolor · snap · 0 tokens
IT EDITS THE REAL FILE

Real code in, real code out.

Designs is smart enough to touch real JS, CSS, and HTML with hidden embeds, and leave what the agent already got right alone. You shape the page, and the file stays a file you can ship.

A · INFINITE CANVAS

The whole page, room to work.

Open the near-done HTML and pan across it like a drafting table. One thing reads red at a time: the element your hand is on. Everything else rests.

B · WON'T BREAK YOUR EMBEDS

It knows what not to touch.

Hidden embeds, scripts, the markup the agent got right. Designs edits around them so a font swap doesn't take your analytics or your forms down with it.

C · LOCAL STUB OR YOUR KEY

Ships with a local-LLM stub that runs on day one. Want more reach? Pipe in your own key through OpenRouter. Your choice, your spend.
