What it does

Open it. Move it. Own it.

No new format, no project lock-in, no export tax. Designs opens the HTML you already have, edits the actual document, and writes it back out clean. The code stays yours from the first click.

01

Open your HTML

Drop in the near-done page your crew built. Designs renders the real DOM on an infinite canvas, not a preview of it.

02

Edit on a real canvas

Click to select, drag blocks where you want them, resize with handles, double-click to type. You move the document itself.

03

Ask a model — if you want

Wire any provider with your own key. The AI applies clean edits to the canvas instead of reloading, so nothing flickers. Skip it entirely and the editor still works.

04

Export clean code

Take the document with inlined styles, or a ZIP of index.html + styles.css + assets. Host it anywhere. No renderer to escape.

05

It knows what not to touch

Hidden embeds, scripts, the markup the agent nailed. Designs edits around them instead of through them — a font swap won't take your analytics, forms, or 3D embeds down with it.

06

The diff is small and readable

You changed a color and a margin, so that's what the export shows. No reformatted file, no surprise rewrite.

FAQ

Honest answers to the obvious questions.

Anything not here, the source is right there to read.

Is it really free?

Yes. MIT, no account, no card, no telemetry. The editor runs on your machine. The only thing you'd ever pay for is a model, and you pay that to your own provider.

Does it ship with an AI model?

It ships with a small local-LLM stub that works the moment you download it. Want more reach? Wire in your own key through any OpenRouter or OpenAI-compatible provider. The AI is optional, not the point.

Will it break my scripts and embeds?

No. Designs edits around hidden embeds, analytics, forms, and 3D objects instead of through them. A font swap won't take your working markup down with it.

Do I own what I export?

Completely. You get plain HTML and CSS, or a ZIP of index.html + styles.css + assets. Host it anywhere. There's no renderer to escape and no lock-in.

How is this different from a page builder?

A builder traps your work behind its own renderer. Designs opens the HTML you already have, edits the real document, and writes it back clean. You move the file, not a copy of it.

Where does it sit in the workflow?

Your crew builds in the Hub, you shape the last ten percent in Designs, then you rest the finished site in Captur'd. One hand passes to the next.
