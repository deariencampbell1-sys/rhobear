Privacy Policy

Effective June 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes how Sun Sponge LLC ("RHOBEAR," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with RHOBEAR Plans, our cloud web application and desktop application (together, the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Service.

1. What RHOBEAR Plans does

RHOBEAR Plans lets you run assistant "agents" and connect your own third-party accounts — such as your email (over IMAP), your calendar, and your Google Drive — so the Service can show you that information and help you act on it. A core principle of the Service is that your content stays with the providers you already use. We connect to those services live on your behalf; we are not a host or archive of your messages, calendar, or files.

2. Information we collect

a. Information you provide

  • Account information — such as your email address and authentication details when you create an account or activate a license.
  • Connection credentials — when you connect a third-party account (e.g., an email mailbox, or your Google account), we store the credential or access token needed to maintain that connection. These are encrypted at rest and are never displayed back to you or shared.
  • Content you create in the Service — such as notes, agent configurations, signatures, and templates you choose to save.

b. Information from connected services

When you connect a service, the Service accesses data from it at your direction and only to provide the features you use — for example, displaying your inbox, reading your calendar's free/busy times, or creating a file in your Google Drive. Except for the encrypted connection credential and any item you explicitly save, we do not retain the content of your connected mailboxes, calendars, or drives. It is read live and rendered to you.

c. Information collected automatically

We may collect limited technical and usage information (such as log data and basic diagnostics) to operate, secure, and improve the Service.

3. Google user data — Limited Use

If you connect a Google account, RHOBEAR Plans requests only the access needed for the features you use, which may include:

  • .../auth/drive.file — to let your agents create and manage only the files the app itself creates in your Google Drive, including documents and the Google Sheets you ask an agent to build. The app cannot see, read, or change any of your other Drive files or spreadsheets.
  • .../auth/calendar and .../auth/calendar.events — to show your availability and manage events you schedule through the Service.

RHOBEAR Plans's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide and improve the user-facing features described above. We do not sell Google user data, do not use it for advertising, and do not allow humans to read it except (a) with your explicit consent, (b) for security purposes (e.g., investigating abuse), (c) to comply with applicable law, or (d) where the data has been aggregated and anonymized. We do not transfer Google user data except as necessary to provide the Service, comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with prior notice.

4. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, secure, and improve the Service;
  • To connect to the third-party services you authorize and perform the actions you request;
  • To communicate with you about your account, security, and support;
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

5. How we share information

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information. We may share information only with:

  • Service providers / subprocessors that help us operate the Service (e.g., hosting, payment processing), bound to confidentiality and permitted to use the information only to provide services to us;

  • Legal and safety recipients, where required by law or to protect rights, property, or safety.

6. How we protect information

Connection credentials and access tokens are encrypted at rest using authenticated encryption, and access is restricted. We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data retention and deletion

We retain account information and saved content for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. When you disconnect a service, we delete the stored credential/token for that connection. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us (see below); we will comply except where retention is required by law or for legitimate business purposes.
