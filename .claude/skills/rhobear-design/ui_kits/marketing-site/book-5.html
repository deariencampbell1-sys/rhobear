MISSION 5.2

Write your first brief.

"You never tell the crew 'build me an app.' You write a brief — and a good brief can't lie."

A brief is a contract. Scope says exactly what you're building. Acceptance criteria says exactly how you'll know it's actually done — not "looks right," but something you can go check with your own eyes. And it stays hold for review — nothing merges until Neo clears it.

MISSION 5.4

Be Neo.

"Fresh eyes. No memory of last week. Just the diff in front of you — and the call is yours."

Neo reads the whole diff, checks the claim against the code, and returns one of three calls: ACCEPT (clean, do what it says), FIX-FORWARD (tiny, obvious fix — just do it and move on), or BOUNCE (send it back with a reason). Review all three below.
