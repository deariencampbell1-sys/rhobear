How it works

It started as a handoff dock. Then it ran everything.

I built Plans so the crew could write each build's path and I could spot drift at a glance. Then I kept adding until it ran the whole back office.

01

Real calendars, not a to-do list

Personal, site, support, billing each get a muted accent. A glance tells you which part of the business a day belongs to.

02

A slot books, the rest fires

The moment a slot is taken, Zoom and email go out. The lead never waits on you to push a button.

03

Money and calendar agree

PayPal and Stripe log every sale inline. You see the booking and the payment as one row, not two systems to reconcile.

04

Talk to the bot

One Telegram bot per calendar. Each answers in plain language and keeps its own log. You run your day without opening the app.

Private by default

Your data stays on your machine.

A local Qwen-4B keeps tokens cheap and reads your email on-device, so nothing leaves your machine and the convenience costs you no privacy. For the big moves, sharp diagrams or always-on crons wired to your own cloud, you reach for Ursa. We do not train on your data.

ON-DEVICE

The model lives with your data

Qwen-4B runs on your box. Your inbox is read where it sits, not shipped to someone else's server to be summarized.

CHEAP

No token meter on the day-to-day

The local model runs the calendar, the bots, and the inbox with no token bill. You only spend credits when you ask the cloud for something big.

YOURS

We don't train on you

No fine-tuning on your calendar, your clients, or your mail. The data is yours, and it stays that way.

Local first, cloud on ask. Ursa is there for the big moves, never the default.

FAQ

Honest answers to the obvious questions.

Anything not here, ping us.

How much is Plans?

Local is a one-time $20 — you own it and host it yourself, no subscription. Cloud is $20/mo, hosted by us. Buy Local once and your first month of Ursa cloud credits is on us.

What's the free first month?

The first time you buy Local, you get a month of Ursa cloud credits free — real credit worth $20, not a trial, and it never auto-charges. Use it up early and switch to pay-as-you-go, or just keep running local for free.

Is there a free version?

You can sign in to the cloud and look around for free, but the calendar, booking, bots, and payments are part of the paid plan — $20 once for Local, or $20/mo for Cloud.

Local or cloud — which?

Pick Local to own it, keep everything on your machine, and get the free first month. Pick Cloud if you'd rather we host it always-on with nothing to install. Cloud users skip the local install and the free-month bonus.

Is my data really private?

Yes. A local Qwen-4B reads your email on-device. The day-to-day never leaves your machine, and we don't train on your data.

What is Ursa?

Our onboard cloud LLM, for the big moves only — sharp diagrams, heavier reasoning, always-on crons. Local users reach for it with pay-as-you-go credits; Cloud includes a monthly allotment. You only spend when you invoke it.

What's a bot per calendar?

One Telegram bot for personal, one for work, one for the business. Each handles its own invites and email and answers in plain language.

Where does Plans sit?

Reviews hands Plans work that already passed the gate. Plans turns it into a running business, then hands the part that answers leads to Sales.
