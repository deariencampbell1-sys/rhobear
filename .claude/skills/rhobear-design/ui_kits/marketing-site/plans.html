WHERE IT STARTED

It began as a handoff dock.

I built Plans so the crew could write each build's path in Mermaid and I could spot drift at a glance, instead of pinging anyone. Then I kept adding until it ran the whole back office.

THE BACK OFFICE

One place runs the parts that used to need five.

Personal Site Support Billing
A · CALENDAR + APPOINTMENTS

Real calendars, not a to-do list.

Personal, site, support, and billing each get their own muted accent, so a glance tells you which part of the business a day belongs to. The live thread is the only thing lit purple.

B · PAYMENTS THAT TRACK

The money and the calendar agree.

PayPal and Stripe forms log every sale inline. You see the booking and the payment as one row, not two systems you reconcile by hand later.

C · BOOKING THAT MATCHES YOUR SITE

A slot books and the rest fires itself.

Booking templates themed to your site. The moment a slot is taken, Zoom and email go out, so the lead never waits on you to push a button.

D · EMAIL, READ ON YOUR MACHINE

Your inbox, surfaced by a local model.

A local model reads your email on-device and brings up what needs you. The reading never leaves your machine, so the convenience costs you no privacy.

TALK TO IT

One Telegram bot per calendar.

One bot for personal, one for work, one for business. Each handles its own invites and email, keeps its own settings log, and answers in plain language. You do not open the app to run your day. You just talk to the bot that owns that part of it.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

Your data stays on your machine.

A local Qwen-4B keeps tokens cheap and reads your email on-device, so nothing leaves your machine. For the big moves, sharp diagrams or always-on crons wired to your own cloud, switch to Ursa, our onboard LLM, on credits or a monthly sub. We do not train on your data.
