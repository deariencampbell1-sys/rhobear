FAQ

Straight answers to the obvious questions.

Anything not here, ping us.

What does a Plans agent actually do?

Real work, not chat. It books the inspection, sends the follow-up, reconciles the payment, and drafts the doc. Anything that touches the outside world stops for your Yes / No / Edit first.

What's a workspace?

One business, with its own agents, connected accounts (PayPal, Gmail, Calendar, GitHub), and memory. Run several side by side — Starter gives you one, Pro three, Business ten.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes — it's the full walkthrough so you can see every agent work. Agents stay off until you upgrade. Free shows you everything; paid turns your agents on.

What counts against my daily limits?

Only two things, each its own separate bucket: actions (generating a draft or sending an email) and website generations (full pages your agent builds). Editing a page you already made is free. Chat, reading, searching, and answers are never metered.

What are Ursa credits?

A $10 pack that adds runs to either bucket — actions or website generations — when you have a busy day. Unused runs roll over, and the pack expires 90 days after your first use of it, not the day you buy it.

What's Arcturus vs Ursa?

They're our two models. Starter runs Arcturus, our everyday model — plenty for day-to-day work. Pro and Business put Ursa, our premium model, on every agent for sharper drafts and tougher reasoning. Ursa credits top up usage when you need more.

Can I run it privately?

Yes. Local is a one-time $20 — Plans on your own machine, fully private, and it never trains on your data. The hosted plans keep your files in your own GitHub or Drive either way.

Do new agents cost extra?

No. As the catalog grows we push new agents to you — calendar, email, payments, research, plus niche trades like roofing, dental, and solar. They count against your plan's agent slots, not a new bill.

Can I get a refund?

No. There are no refunds on the cloud plan or on credits, and no upgrade-then-cancel for money back — when payment ends, paid access simply ends. That's why the walkthrough is free: spend real time there, then turn your agents on only when you're sure.
