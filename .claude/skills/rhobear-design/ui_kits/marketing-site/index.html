RHOBEAR DesignsOpen source · MIT

You build ninety percent with your crew, same as always. The last ten is where the tokens bleed out, nudging one pixel at a time. So open the near-done HTML on an infinite canvas and finish that last ten by hand: drag it where you want it, swap a font, fix the one color that's off, with no round-trip to the model. It's smart enough to touch real JS, CSS, and HTML with hidden embeds without wrecking what the agent already got right. Ships with a local-LLM stub that works the moment you download it, or pipe in your own key through OpenRouter. The file stays yours, MIT, forever.

Keep it clean
RHOBEAR ReviewsGitHub App

You're paying for hundreds of reviews a month, and your own agents are no help: drop one into a live thread and it drags last week's context into this week's PR. Reviews fixes both. It spins up a fresh reviewer on every pull request and throws it away after, so old context never carries over, but it still knows your code, reading the whole repo as a graph with a second model grading it over its shoulder. You get an honest verdict that's more than a diff before anything merges. Built in, Verifies maps your app's pathways and flags dead code, dead routes, and the tokens you waste on them, three full app-maps a month, so you stop rebasing through a graveyard or shipping the old UI again.

Take it to market
RHOBEAR PlansWeb · local-first

Run your business in the same place your agents read it. I built Plans as a handoff dock first: the crew writes each build's path in Mermaid, so I can spot drift at a glance instead of pinging anyone. Then I kept adding until it ran the whole back office. Calendar and appointments. PayPal and Stripe forms that track every sale. Booking templates that match your site. Zoom and email that fire the second a slot is booked. One Telegram bot per calendar, one each for personal, work, and business, handling your invites and email. You just talk to it. A local Qwen-4B keeps tokens cheap and reads your email on-device, so nothing leaves your machine. For the big moves, sharp diagrams or always-on crons wired to your own cloud, switch to Ursa, our onboard LLM. We don't train on your data.
