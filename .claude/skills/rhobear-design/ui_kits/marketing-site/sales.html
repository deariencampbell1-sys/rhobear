FAQ

What size clients do you take?

Both. A solo founder who needs one always-on agent, and a company that needs a whole private stack. We scope to the job, not to your headcount.

What does it cost?

Custom work is quoted to the build — one flat, honest number after we scope it. Our own apps have real free tiers and clear prices; the bespoke work is priced to what it takes.

Yes. We stand up private inference on your own secure servers with a no-training floor, HIPAA-grade options included. Nothing has to touch a third-party cloud.
