NEW · RHO ON THE DESKTOP

A talking orb that lives on your taskbar.

The desktop companion puts Rho on your Windows machine: a living orb you can drag anywhere, click to expand into the full surface — type, talk, upload, camera — and it attaches to the RHOBEAR Hub when one is running. With the Hub, Rho can drive your RHOBEAR apps. Without it, Rho is your research companion: it talks, digs the web, and reads your files and docs — and it never writes without the harness. The orb's little dot tells you which mode you're in.

Shipping alongside the Hub. Until then, Rho is one click away on every cloud surface.
