LESSON 0.1

The person who didn't learn to code.

"He didn't learn to write software. He learned to lead the fleet that writes it."

There's a difference between a coder and a conductor. A coder writes the notes. A conductor gets an orchestra to play them — and never touches an instrument. This whole course makes you the conductor. The AI crew are the musicians; they know the notes cold. Your job is taste, direction, and knowing what "good" sounds like.

And here's the proof it works. Every dollar it took to build RHOBEAR — a seven-product company — fit on a stack of receipts you can hold. Touch one.

LESSON 0.2

What "vibe coding" actually is.

"You've talked to an AI. That's 80% of the skill. Here's the other 20%."

Vibe coding isn't magic and it isn't the AI. It's a loop you run: you say what you want in plain language → the crew makes a swing → you look at what came back → you correct → repeat. You never touch syntax. You touch direction. The whole game is knowing what you want and recognizing when you've got it.

LESSON 0.3

Meet your crew.

"You're not getting one assistant. You're getting a whole team — each with exactly one job."

These aren't chatbots wearing hats. They're roles, each with one job and a rule about what it never does. That's the secret to shipping like a team of eight: nobody does everything, so nothing turns to spaghetti. Tap a crew member to see how they work.

LESSON 0.4

The promise — and the honest limits.

"Here's what this makes possible. And here's what it will never do for you."

You can build real products, run a real business, earn real money. You cannot skip taste, skip judgment, or skip caring — the AI amplifies you, it doesn't replace you. And you are always the pilot. Every crew member has an autonomy dial: how much rope you give it. Slide it and watch who's driving.

◈ Scar — a real one
The day "done" wasn't done.

A change merged. Every check was green. But the live product still showed the old thing — nobody had opened it with their own eyes. A whole day burned. The lesson you'll carry the whole way: a green checkmark isn't proof. You saw it work like a real user, or it isn't done.
